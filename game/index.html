THE QUIET GALLERY

Pip the courier delivered the wrong parcel to the wrong museum — and it is locked in the vault wing after closing. Slip in after dark, pocket the three relics that prove the parcel is yours, and reach the glowing exit without ever being caught.

Wardens patrol with lanterns and watchful eyes: their yellow cones are what they can see. Crates block their sight, planters hide you completely, and a thrown pebble pulls a warden away to investigate. Sneak with Shift to move slowly and quietly. If the meter fills, the alarm sounds and every warden hunts you — break line of sight and wait it out.
